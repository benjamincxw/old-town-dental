Why Old Town Dental

A general practice, done properly

Positioned as a one-stop clinic for curative and preventive dentistry — here's what that means for you.

One stop, curative and preventive

From routine examinations and cleanings to fillings and extractions, the clinic covers general dental care under one roof, so you're not shuttled between specialists for everyday needs.

Root canal treatment on-site

Root canal treatment is one of the clinic's core services — a damaged or infected tooth can often be treated and saved here, rather than referred elsewhere.

Open every day of the week

No weekly closing day — unlike many single-dentist clinics, Old Town Dental is open daily, so a toothache doesn't have to wait for the right day to be seen.

Services

General dentistry, start to finish

The core treatments offered at the clinic. Ask on WhatsApp if you're not sure which one you need.

Examination & Consultation

Routine check-ups and diagnosis to catch problems early and plan the right treatment.

Scaling & Polishing

Professional cleaning that removes plaque, tartar and surface stains between check-ups.

Fillings & Restorations

Repairing decayed or damaged teeth to restore shape, strength and everyday function.

Root Canal Treatment

Treating infected or badly damaged teeth from the inside, to save them from extraction.

Extractions

Safe removal of teeth that can't be saved or are causing pain and complications.

Dentures

Custom-fitted removable replacements for one or more missing teeth.
